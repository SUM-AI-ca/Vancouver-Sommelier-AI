* **Sparkling Sakes:** The lineup also features sparkling options, such as the crisp *Junmai Sparkling* and the dry, traditional-method *Mirai* sparkling sake.
* **Food Pairing:** Designed with West Coast food culture in mind, OSAKE is crafted to pair naturally with local seafood (such as fresh oysters), seasonal vegetables, and lighter, ingredient-led dishes.
