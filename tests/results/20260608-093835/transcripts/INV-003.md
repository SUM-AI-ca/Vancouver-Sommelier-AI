### **Wine: Tantalus Old Vines Riesling**
**Producer & Region:** Tantalus Vineyards, East Kelowna, Okanagan Valley, BC VQA.
**The Details:** This is widely considered one of Canada’s premier white wines. It is sourced from the estate's historic vinifera blocks planted in 1978 (specifically Clone 21B), with the majority of the fruit coming from "Block 5," which is sheltered by mature Ponderosa pines. The wine is celebrated for its intense concentration, searing acidity, deep minerality, and exceptional aging potential.
