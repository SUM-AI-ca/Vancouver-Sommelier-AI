### 4. Sake

#### Why This Pairing Works
Sake is naturally high in **succinic acid (umami)**, which creates a synergistic flavor explosion when paired with the soy sauce, garlic, and beef proteins in Galbi. Unlike wine, sake has very low acidity and no tannins, meaning it won't clash with the sweetness of the marinade; instead, it embraces it.

#### Recommended Style
*   **Junmai or Yamahai/Kimoto:** These styles are richer, more rustic, and have higher natural acidity and earthy umami notes that can stand up to heavy, grilled red meats. They can be served chilled or slightly warmed.
